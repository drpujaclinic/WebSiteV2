FAQ

Questions, Answered

Can other members see my phone number?
No. This is a broadcast-style channel, so your number is never visible to other members — not even to us. You can follow along completely privately.
Will I get spammed with messages?
No. It's one-way — you'll only receive the updates we post. You won't be added to group chats, and no one can message you directly.
Is the community free to join?
Yes. Joining and following the community is completely free, always.
Will I receive a personal fertility consultation through the group?
No. This community is for general education only. For personal diagnosis or treatment, please book a consultation with Dr. Puja or another qualified specialist.
Can I share my medical reports in the group?
We recommend not sharing personal medical reports or test results in the group, since it's a shared educational space rather than a private clinical channel. Bring them to a consultation instead.
Is this only for women?
No. Partners, couples, and anyone interested in fertility education are welcome to join.
Can doctors and other healthcare professionals join?
Yes. The community also welcomes primary-care doctors and healthcare providers who want to strengthen their fertility knowledge.
Does joining guarantee a pregnancy or a specific outcome?
No. This community offers education and support — it does not guarantee any medical outcome. Individual results depend on personal clinical evaluation and care.

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026 · DPDP Act 2023 Compliant

Dr. Puja's Clinic ("we", "our", or "Clinic") is committed to protecting the privacy and security of your personal data. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices in compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023 of India ("the DPDP Act").

1. Data We Collect

When you use our online booking form, join our WhatsApp Fertility Community, or otherwise interact with us digitally, we may collect: Full Name, Contact Phone Number, Email Address, and Health Reason / Purpose of Consultation (provided voluntarily).

2. Purpose of Collection & Processing

Your personal information is processed solely for explicit, specific, and legitimate purposes: facilitating appointment bookings, sending clinic communications (confirmations, reminders, rescheduling alerts), answering direct inquiries, and operating our free educational WhatsApp community.

3. Cookies and Analytics — Your Consent Choice

We use Google Analytics (GA4) to understand website usage patterns (pages visited, session duration, approximate location via IP). We only activate analytics cookies after you provide explicit consent via the cookie banner shown on your first visit — no analytics tracking occurs before that choice is made. You can withdraw or change your consent at any time by clearing your browser's local storage for this site, or by contacting us. Declining consent does not affect your ability to use the website, book an appointment, or join our WhatsApp community. Analytics data is pseudonymised and used only to improve the website experience — never combined with your medical or booking data.

4. WhatsApp Fertility Community — How Your Number Is Handled

  • The community operates as a broadcast-style WhatsApp Channel. Your phone number is not visible to other members, and other members cannot see who else has joined.
  • Your number is used solely to deliver community content, subject to WhatsApp's own platform terms — never for marketing outside the channel, and never sold or shared with third parties.
  • Joining the community is entirely separate from your clinical records. Please do not share personal medical reports or test results within the community — see our Terms of Service for community guidelines.
  • You may leave the community at any time by leaving the WhatsApp channel directly.

5. Children's Data and Parental Consent

In accordance with the DPDP Act's protections for children (individuals under 18 years of age), we do not knowingly process personal data of, or accept appointment bookings for, anyone under 18 without the verifiable consent of a parent or lawful guardian. If you are booking an appointment on behalf of a minor, you confirm that you are their parent or lawful guardian and consent to this processing on their behalf. If we become aware that we have collected data from a minor without appropriate parental/guardian consent, we will take steps to delete such data promptly.

6. Data Sharing and Third-Party Processors

Beyond Google Analytics (see Section 3), we do not sell, rent, or otherwise share your personal data with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

7. Data Retention Period

We retain your digital data only as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described above, or to comply with applicable medical record retention regulations under Indian healthcare law.

8. Your Rights as a Data Principal

Under the DPDP Act, you have the following rights, exercisable at any point:

  • Right to Access: Obtain a summary of the personal data we process about you.
  • Right to Correction & Erasure: Correct inaccuracies, or request erasure of personal data no longer necessary for the purpose it was collected.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw your consent for digital communications or analytics tracking at any point, without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.
  • Right to Grievance Redressal: Raise a complaint regarding our data processing with our Grievance Officer (Section 10) before escalating further.
  • Right to Nominate: Nominate another individual to exercise these rights on your behalf in the event of your death or incapacity.

9. Your Duties as a Data Principal

Under Section 15 of the DPDP Act, 2023, as a Data Principal you also have certain duties, including to:

  • Comply with applicable laws when exercising your rights under the DPDP Act;
  • Not impersonate another person when providing personal data for a specified purpose;
  • Not suppress material information while providing personal data;
  • Not register a false or frivolous grievance or complaint with us or with the Data Protection Board of India;
  • Furnish only information that is verifiably authentic when exercising your right to correction or erasure.

10. Grievance Officer / Contact
